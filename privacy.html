Privacy Notice

This notice explains how personal data is handled when you use the Between the Walls website, register for the optional immersive investigation experience, or participate in it.

Effective: 31 August 2026 · Controller: Filip Dimitrievski / Cosmic Films · North Macedonia

1. Controller and contact

For this website and Experience, the personal-data controller is Filip Dimitrievski, operating the Between the Walls project under the production name Cosmic Films.

Privacy contact: cosmicfilmprodukcija@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

  • name, display name, email address and selected age;
  • your confirmation that you are 18 or older;
  • account identifiers and authentication/session information;
  • game progress, assigned-poster information, QR scans, completed stages, timestamps and reward eligibility once those features are enabled;
  • messages you send to us;
  • technical data used for hosting and security, such as IP address, browser/device information, request logs and timestamps.

Your account password is handled through Supabase Auth and is not available to us in readable form.

3. Why we use it

  • to create and secure your account;
  • to confirm eligibility for the 18+ Experience;
  • to save investigation progress and determine which assigned posters advance your account;
  • to prevent abuse, cheating and unauthorized access;
  • to send necessary verification, security or reward-related messages;
  • to fulfil rewards where applicable;
  • to respond to requests and maintain the website and Experience;
  • to comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal bases

Where required by applicable law, we rely on: performance of the service and these Terms where processing is necessary to provide the account and Experience; legitimate interests for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting; legal obligations where processing is required by law; and consent for optional processing or communication where consent is legally required.

We do not use your registration email for unrelated advertising or marketing unless you separately choose to receive it.

5. 18+ information

The Experience is intended only for adults. We use the age information and 18+ confirmation you provide to enforce that restriction. We do not knowingly permit people under 18 to register.

6. Service providers and recipients

We use Supabase for database and authentication services and Netlify for website hosting and delivery. These providers may process technical and account data on our behalf under their own contractual and security arrangements.

Where needed to fulfil a reward, we may share only the information reasonably necessary with the relevant screening organizer, platform or reward partner. We do not sell your personal data.

7. International processing

Some service providers may process or store data outside North Macedonia. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we rely on appropriate contractual or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms made available by those providers.

8. Browser storage and cookies

The website may use essential browser storage or similar technologies to keep you signed in, maintain authentication sessions and support security. We do not intentionally use advertising cookies. If non-essential analytics or marketing technologies are added later, this notice will be updated and any legally required consent mechanism will be provided first.

9. Retention

We keep account and Experience-progress data while your account is active and, unless a longer period is legally required, for no longer than 12 months after the public Experience has ended. You may request deletion earlier.

Security and technical logs are kept only for the period reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention, subject to provider retention practices. Information needed for reward fulfilment, accounting, disputes or legal compliance may be retained for the period required by law. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a limited backup-retention period before being overwritten.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to be informed about processing, access your data, correct it, request deletion, request restriction, receive certain data in a portable format, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis, and complain to the Agency for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of North Macedonia.

To exercise a privacy right, email cosmicfilmprodukcija@gmail.com. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account before acting on it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service, including authentication and access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so you should use a strong, unique password and keep your credentials private.

12. Changes

We may update this notice when the Experience, service providers or legal requirements change. The current version will be published here with an updated effective date.

13. Contact and complaints

Privacy questions and requests: cosmicfilmprodukcija@gmail.com.

You may also contact the Agency for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of North Macedonia if you believe your rights have been violated.
